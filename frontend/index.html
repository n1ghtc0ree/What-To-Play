How to load your Steam library

You can load your Steam library by entering your Steam ID or vanity URL in the field below.

Important: Your Steam profile must be public and your game activity visible, otherwise Steam API will restrict access to your games list.

How to find your Steam ID / Vanity URL:

  1. Go to your Steam profile
  2. Look at the URL - it will show either a custom name (vanity URL) or a 17-digit number (Steam ID)
  3. Copy and paste it into the input field
  4. Click the checkmark button to fetch your library
